How a browser reads this

CSP3 walks a fallback chain per resource kind: script checks ask script-src-elem (elements) or script-src-attr (attributes), then script-src, then default-src. Workers walk worker-src → child-src → script-src → default-src. form-action, base-uri and frame-ancestors never fall back - absent means unrestricted. Inside a source list: 'none' wins over everything (even combined with 'self'), *.example.com matches subdomains but not the bare domain, http: also matches https (never the reverse), and 'self' is the exact scheme + host + port of your origin. Duplicate directives are ignored after the first.
